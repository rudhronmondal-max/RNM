01 — ABOUT

Research begins
with curiosity.

I am a Materials Science and Engineering graduate from Khulna University of Engineering & Technology (KUET), currently pursuing an M.Sc. in Materials Science and Engineering at the University of Augsburg, Germany.

My academic interests centre on computational materials science, energy materials, perovskite and antiperovskite compounds, and the development of materials for optoelectronic and energy-conversion applications. I am particularly interested in using first-principles calculations and device-level simulations to understand how composition and structure influence material performance.
